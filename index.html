What does electrical protection do?

Protection systems monitor electrical conditions, identify faults, command circuit breakers to isolate the affected section and record the event. They do not generate electricity or remove every cause of outages.

Relevant to oil and gas and industry

Substations, pump and compressor drives, transformers, motors and on-site generation need properly engineered protection and control. Russian manufacturers publish product ranges for these applications.

Each facility requires engineering, interface checks and owner approval. Hazardous-area requirements must be specified separately. This is not a claim that every product suits every application.
